FIELD NOTES — LEARN BEFORE YOU DESCEND

Read. Roll. Strike.
Amber shapes show where the next attack will land. Roll as the strike begins; only the middle of your roll is invincible. Your sword automatically faces the Basilisk’s head.

Make every opening count.
Attack its head when it glows pale green after a move. Scales resist damage at other times. Swinging and rolling use stamina. Stop attacking to recover it.

The chamber is your shield.
Stone pillars block the lethal gaze. Venom stays on the floor. Healing takes one second and a hit interrupts it. Below half health, the Basilisk attacks faster.
