THE FIELD GUIDE · KNOW WHAT WILL KILL YOU

Gold tracks. White commits.
Eight stone pillars block lunges and sight attacks; they also shelter you from the Basilisk’s bite. Venom pools still hurt behind cover. Warnings follow you at first. When the outline turns white, the attack is locked. Dodge late. Only the middle 0.18 seconds of a roll is invincible. Attacks, healing, and buffs cannot be cancelled.

A pause is not always an opening.
The chain counter tells you how many strikes remain. Attack on the green recovery ring. Heavy attacks break posture faster. Keep 31 stamina for your escape; mashing attacks will leave you exposed.

Choose your magic.
Q activates your weapon’s buff for 25 stamina. Buffs have cooldowns. You have 140 health. E uses one of four healing draughts and restores up to 60 health after 1.2s. Getting hit interrupts it. Supplies refill between bosses. Defeat the Basilisk for +15% damage, then the Dementor for +30% total damage. These bonuses last through retries until you start a new campaign or reload.
